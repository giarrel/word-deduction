# English

App name: Word Deduction

Short description: One phone, secret words and your group. Play a quick game or go classic.

## Full description

Pass one phone around. Everyone gets a secret word – but some words are different. Give clues, listen closely and decide together who is bluffing.

Word Deduction is a word and bluffing game for people in the same place. Add your names, choose a mode and play. No account, ads, purchases or internet connection needed.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY

• Quick: from 3 players. One clue round, one vote and the result. For short games and groups that keep changing.
• Classic: from 4 players. Eliminate one suspect after each vote and continue with the survivors. From 5 players, add Mr. White: no word, plenty of bluffing, and one chance to guess the majority word after being eliminated.

LESS SETUP, MORE CONVERSATION

• Up to 20 active players and 40 saved names.
• Your group stays saved. Rename people, pause them, let them rejoin or remove them. Undo an accidental removal.
• Pull up the card or hold the reveal area to read your word. Release to hide it immediately.
• Give clues and vote together aloud. The phone guides the next step and records your decision.
• Results show the words, roles and why the winning side won. Start the next match with one tap.
• Resume an interrupted match with every secret covered.

WORDS INCLUDED FROM THE START

520 word pairs across 20 everyday themes are already included. Played pairs do not repeat until the collection has been used. Menus, rules and words are available in English and German.

Everyone shares one phone. There is no online mode. Your group discusses and votes in person; the app does not count individual ballots.

## What's new in 1.0.0

The first Android release includes Quick and Classic modes, optional Mr. White, saved groups and 520 English and German word pairs. Play offline, pull up cards to reveal words and resume safely after an interruption.
