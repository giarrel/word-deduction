# English

App name: Word Deduction

Short description: One phone, secret words and three ways to bluff with your group.

## Full description

Pass one phone around. Everyone reads their secret card. Most share a word; some get a different one, and Mr. White gets no word at all. Give clues, listen closely and decide together who is bluffing.

Word Deduction is a word and bluffing game for people in the same place. Add your names, choose a mode and play. No account, ads, purchases or internet connection needed.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY

• Quick: from 3 players. After one clue round, select and confirm suspects one at a time. Catch every adversary; accuse a Civilian and the adversaries win. Your group decides how much to discuss between selections.
• Classic: from 4 players. Find suspects, reveal eliminated roles and play new clue rounds with the survivors.
• Kings: from 5 players. Protect your hidden leader and find the opposing King. Ordinary players know their word and leader, but not their side. The app suggests a surviving starter for each clue round. Mr. White is the evil King. If caught, he gets one last chance: guess the good word or identify the good King. Adjust the number of his Undercover teammates within your group's limits.

Quick and Classic let you choose the Undercover and Mr. White counts, including games without Undercover. Each caught Mr. White gets one immediate guess at the Civilian word. A correct guess wins for all Mr. Whites together. Your group judges the spoken answer; the word stays hidden until the result.

LESS SETUP, MORE CONVERSATION

• Up to 20 people play together.
• Names and order stay saved. Use Edit to rename or remove someone, add new names, and drag a handle to change the order. Undo an accidental removal.
• Role preferences are remembered per mode and temporarily adjusted for smaller groups. Auto restores suggested counts.
• Pull up the card or hold the reveal area to read your word. Release to hide it immediately.
• Give clues and vote together aloud. The phone guides the next step and records your decision.
• Results show the words, roles and why the winning side won. Start the next match with one tap.
• Resume an interrupted match with every secret covered.

WORDS INCLUDED FROM THE START

520 word pairs across 20 everyday themes are already included. Played pairs do not repeat until the collection has been used. Menus, rules and words are available in English and German.

Everyone shares one phone. There is no online mode. Your group discusses and votes in person; the app does not count individual ballots.
